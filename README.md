# PanelVerse

PanelVerse is an Android and iOS app for browsing manga and comics, keeping a reading list, and picking up where you left off.

### Install on Android

Download the APK directly on your phone or tablet.

1. When the download finishes, tap the file in your notifications or find it under **Files → Downloads**.
2. If Android asks for permission to install from your browser or Files app, tap **Settings** and turn on **Allow from this source** for that app. Go back and tap **Install**, then **Open**. You can turn the permission off after installing. [Android installation help](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/7391672)

## Features

- **Home & Search** — Browse Manga, Marvel, DC, Invincible, and Comics, or search by title with fast cover loading and Discover recommendations.
- **Series & Library** — Browse manga chapters (including Arabic editions where available) and comic issues, share series links, and organize **Favorites**, **Reading**, **Want to Read**, and **Finished**.
- **Native Reader** — Fast page loading with parallel preloading, scrubber controls, pinch/double-tap zoom, and right-to-left, left-to-right, or vertical reading modes.
- **Ad Filtering** — Toggle **Reader options → Block ads** to block known ad hosts and hide supported overlays.
- **Offline Library** — Download supported chapters into local CBZ archives or import your own CBZ files (up to 250 MB).
- **Privacy & Account Controls** — Paper and ink themes, local reading reminders, opt-in in-app messages, offline Privacy Policy / Terms of Use / Copyright & Takedown viewers, and self-service **Delete Account** under **Settings → Account**.
